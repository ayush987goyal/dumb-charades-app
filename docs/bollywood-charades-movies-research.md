# Bollywood movies for Dumb Charades: research and curated catalog

This reference guide establishes design rules and a verified catalog of 600 unique Bollywood films built specifically for Dumb Charades (acting and miming without speaking). Every entry is selected so players can either break the title down word-by-word using standard Indian charades vocabulary or perform an unmistakable signature hook step, prop action, or character posture.

---

## 1. Primary and community sources consulted

The catalog and selection rules synthesize historical box office data, film encyclopedias, and Indian party-game conventions:

1. **Historical box office records (Box Office India & trade archives, 1950 to 2026)**
   - Box Office India decade-wise "All Time Blockbuster", "Blockbuster", and "Super Hit" classifications from the 1950s through the 2010s ([boxofficeindia.com](https://www.boxofficeindia.com/)).
   - Annual highest-grossing Hindi film charts on Wikipedia and trade trackers (Bollywood Hungama, Sacnilk, Koimoi) covering theatrical and streaming releases from 1970 through 2026, including recent theatrical hits such as *Jawan* (2023), *Stree 2* (2024), *Bhool Bhulaiyaa 3* (2024), *Chhaava* (2025), *Sitaare Zameen Par* (2025), *Dhurandhar* (2025), and *Border 2* (2026) ([en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Indian_films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Indian_films)).
2. **Cultural & audience popularity indexes (IMDb India & Wikipedia filmographies)**
   - IMDb Top 250 Indian Movies and decade-specific popular Hindi cinema lists, used to retain cult comedies (*Andaz Apna Apna*, *Hera Pheri*, *Chupke Chupke*, *Jaane Bhi Do Yaaro*, *Khosla Ka Ghosla*, *Go Goa Gone*, *Madgaon Express*) that outperformed their initial box office run on television and OTT platforms.
3. **Indian Dumb Charades community canons & tournament lists**
   - Curated charades decks and discussion threads from `r/bollywood` on Reddit, DesiBoardGames, MensXP, FilmAdda, ScoopWhoop, and ZEE5 party guides, which document both reliable team warm-ups and classic multi-word "stumper" titles (*Andheri Raat Mein Diya Tere Haath Mein*, *Jal Bin Machhli Nritya Bin Bijli*, *Matru Ki Bijlee Ka Mandola*, *Ghode Ko Jalebi Khilane Le Ja Riya Hoon*, *Luv Shuv Tey Chicken Khurana*).

## 2. Selection criteria: what makes a Bollywood movie work in Dumb Charades

A movie that is great to watch is not automatically great to mime. In Indian Dumb Charades, players communicate using two primary channels: **word-by-word vocabulary decomposition** and **whole-movie cultural shorthand**. Every title in this catalog passes three strict filters.

### Rule A: Multi-word titles built on concrete Hindi or English root words
Multi-word titles are the backbone of Dumb Charades because the actor can hold up fingers for the word count, jump directly to the easiest physical word, and chain the remaining words together. We prioritize titles containing high-frequency concrete tokens:
- **Body parts and senses:** *Dil* (beating heart on chest), *Aankh* (pointing to eyes), *Haath* (waving palms), *Kaan* (pulling ear), *Sar / Bheja* (tapping skull), *Chehra* (circling face), *Honth* (pointing to lips), *Kadam / Paon* (marching feet).
- **People, roles, and kinship:** *Raja / Rani / Badshah / Shahenshah* (placing crown on head), *Chor / Daku* (tiptoeing with mask or loot bag), *Police / Sipahi / Inspector* (saluting or twirling baton), *Dulha / Dulhan / Biwi / Pati* (putting on veil or garland), *Baap / Maa / Beta / Bhai / Behen* (cradling baby or tying rakhi), *Coolie* (lifting luggage on head), *bawarchi* (stirring pot).
- **Tangible objects, animals, and places:** *Ghar / Makaan* (forming a roof with hands), *Roti / Kapda* (kneading dough / pulling shirt), *Phool* (smelling a flower), *Khat / Chिट्ठी* (writing on palm), *Train / Gadi / Taxi / Bus* (pulling train whistle or steering wheel), *Bhoot / Stree / Aatma* (dangling wrists and haunting face), *Sher / Bhediya / Bandar / Haathi / Ghoda / Machhli* (animal pantomimes).
- **Numbers and quantities:** *Ek, Do, Teen, Chaar, Paanch, Saat, Das, Solah, Bees, Chalis, Sau, Hazar, Crore* (counted directly on fingers) and pairs of opposites (*Kabhi / Kabhie*, *Khushi / Gham*, *Thoda / Zyada*, *Aaj / Kal*, *Din / Raat*, *Chota / Bada*).

### Rule B: Strict gatekeeping for single-word titles
Single-word titles are notoriously frustrating if the word is an abstract emotion or a proper noun with no visual hook. A single-word movie is permitted in this catalog **only** if it satisfies at least one of two conditions:
1. **Concrete physical noun or verb:** The word itself is an everyday object, animal, food, or physical action that can be mimed without knowing the film (for example: *Dhol*, *Ludo*, *Thappad*, *Kathal*, *Bhediya*, *Queen*, *Cocktail*, *Jalebi*, *Piku* via constipation/badminton, *Barfi!* via sweet-cutting, *Kites*, *Taxi*, *Train*, *Koyla*, *Suhaag*, *Trishul*, *Deewar*, *Zanjeer*).
2. **Universally recognized cultural signature:** The movie has an iconic hook step, character posture, or prop routine known across generations of Indian players (for example: *Sholay* via Thakur's amputated arms or Gabbar's belt, *Lagaan* via cricket batting in a dhoti, *Dangal* via thigh-slapping wrestling stance, *Devdas* via bottle drinking and dramatic collapse, *Ghajini* via Polaroid photos and head scar, *PK* via wide eyes and transistor radio, *Don* via collar-pop and gun pose, *Krrish* via superhero mask and leap, *Pushpa* via chin-hand swipe).

### Rule C: Explicit exclusions
To keep gameplay fast and prevent dead rounds where the actor can only resort to syllabic charades ("sounds like"), we exclude:
- **Abstract single-word Urdu, Persian, or Sanskrit nouns:** Titles such as *Guzaarish*, *Fitoor*, *Kalank*, *Tashan*, *Shiddat*, *Gehraiyaan*, *Aazaad*, *Ishaqzaade*, *Kurbaan*, *Fanaa*, or *Fiza* offer no direct physical object to mime and force tedious phonetic spelling.
- **Arbitrary legal sections, dates, or bare numbers:** Titles like *Article 15*, *Section 375*, *Article 370*, *Dhara 302*, *NH10*, or *1920* turn the game into a finger-counting guessing exercise with no acting payoff.
- **Obscure box-office washouts without charades fame:** Unless a flop became a staple of Indian Dumb Charades precisely because its literal wording is funny and decomposable (covered in Section 3.6), unmemorable theatrical releases are omitted.
- **Redundant numbered sequels:** If *Housefull*, *Golmaal*, *Dhoom*, *Welcome*, *Singham*, *Fukrey*, *Stree*, or *Bhool Bhulaiyaa* is already in the deck, adding *Housefull 2*, *Housefull 3*, *Housefull 4*, and *Housefull 5* dilutes variety unless a specific sequel has a distinct subtitle (*Golmaal Again*, *Tiger Zinda Hai*, *Baahubali 2: The Conclusion*).
